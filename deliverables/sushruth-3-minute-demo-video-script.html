Sushruth — 3-Minute Maximum Demo Video Script

Target runtime: 2 minutes 50 seconds Format: Screen recording with voice-over Rule: Preload demo data and keep every page open in browser tabs before recording.

0:00–0:12 — Opening

On screen: Sushruth logo → multilingual landing page → sign-in.

Voice-over:

“This is Sushruth—an accessibility-first healthcare companion that converts fragmented patient information into a structured, doctor-reviewable health journey.”

0:12–0:28 — Onboarding and Accessibility

On screen: Profile setup → language selector → large-text and screen-reader controls → dashboard.

Voice-over:

“After secure sign-in, patients create their health profile and receive a permanent, privacy-safe Sushruth ID. The interface supports multilingual navigation, browser speech, large text, and voice, tap, or typed input.”

0:28–1:05 — Adaptive Pre-Consultation

On screen: Start Pre-Consultation → enter “chest pain” → rapidly show adaptive questions → medical history → AYUSH fields.

Voice-over:

“Pre-Consultation begins with the chief complaint. For chest pain, Sushruth asks complaint-specific questions about onset, severity, character, radiation, breathlessness, exertion, sweating, and dizziness. It then captures medications, allergies, past and family history, lifestyle, review of systems, and all twelve AYUSH Dashavidha parameters.”

On screen: Select a severe red-flag response.

Voice-over:

“Critical responses trigger immediate call-108 or seek-emergency-care guidance. Sushruth does not diagnose or dispatch an ambulance.”

1:05–1:28 — Reports, Prescriptions, and Health Records

On screen: Upload a report → show extracted facts → upload prescription → records timeline.

Voice-over:

“Patients can upload reports and prescriptions. AI assists in extracting medicines, test results, and clinical facts, while keeping them available for human verification. The Health Records area organises documents, prescriptions, and previous consultations in one place.”

1:28–1:48 — Daily Health Management

On screen: Quick montage of tracker, medications, appointments, and child vaccination.

Voice-over:

“Beyond one consultation, Sushruth supports health tracking, medication schedules, appointment records, prescription management, and child-vaccination tracking based on recognised schedules.”

1:48–2:08 — Care Circle and Caretakers

On screen: Settings → copy Sushruth ID → Care Circle request → recipient accepts → active member list → caretaker page.

Voice-over:

“Using a permanent Sushruth ID, patients can send a Care Circle request. Access begins only after acceptance, and the ID itself is never a credential. Caretaker workflows help families coordinate daily care without exposing records to unapproved users.”

2:08–2:36 — Patient-to-Doctor Handoff

On screen: Patient reviews summary and submits → switch to doctor dashboard → open submission → review sections → add note → mark reviewed.

Voice-over:

“Before submission, the patient reviews the generated summary and confirms consent. The case enters the assigned doctor’s queue. The clinician reviews patient-reported history, AYUSH data, and extracted report facts, adds notes, and marks the submission reviewed or incomplete. AI prepares information; the doctor makes the clinical decision.”

2:36–2:50 — Privacy, Scope, and Closing

On screen: Privacy settings → Emergency Help showing “Feature under development” → final dashboard montage.

Voice-over:

“Clerk authentication, server-side ownership checks, and private file storage protect access. Emergency alerts, live ABDM, ABHA, FHIR, and hospital integration remain future scope. Sushruth’s purpose is clear: less time collecting scattered information, and more time focused on patient care.”

End card: Sushruth — Structured before consultation. Human-verified before care.

Recording Checklist

• Use a prepared demo patient; never show real health information.
• Keep the browser zoom consistent and hide bookmarks and notifications.
• Pre-upload one sample report and one prescription.
• Pre-create a second demo account for Care Circle acceptance.
• Pre-create one pending case for the doctor dashboard.
• Use fast cuts for tracker, medications, appointments, vaccination, and settings.
• Do not wait for AI generation on camera; cut from submission to the completed result.
• Keep the Emergency Help screen visible for no more than two seconds.
• Record the voice-over separately and edit the screen capture to match it.
• Export at 1080p with readable cursor highlights and subtitles.
