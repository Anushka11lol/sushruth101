Document overview

This document describes the functionality currently present in the Sushruth AI healthcare assistant. It separates implemented workflows from important boundaries and avoids presenting AI output as diagnosis or medical advice.

Latest Health Tracker change: the stress-level slider has been removed. New daily logs retain date, water, sleep, activity, mood and symptoms. No hidden default stress score is submitted. Older stress values remain readable in historical entries.

1. Authentication and portal selection

Authentication

Entry workflow
Open Sushruth→Signed in?→Authenticate→Portal role→Patient onboarding or Doctor dashboard

2. Patient onboarding

Wellness questionnaire

The initial questionnaire covers age group, known conditions, current feeling, sleep, general stress, energy, eating habits, physical activity, mental-wellbeing factors and the patient’s main health goal.

Its AI wellness snapshot can include a general summary, daily recommendations, sleep/stress/energy observations, goal reminder and an overall wellness score. Questionnaire answers and analysis are cached in the user’s browser.

Language onboarding

  • Selection from 22 Indian languages
  • Native-script language names
  • Per-user persistence
  • RTL handling for Kashmiri, Urdu and other configured RTL languages
Patient onboarding
Patient portal→Wellness questionnaire→AI wellness snapshot→Language selection→Dashboard

5. Patient dashboard

The dashboard reads current information from application APIs and summarizes:

  • Recent triage sessions
  • Upcoming appointments
  • Active medications
  • Care Circle alerts
  • Wellness information and personalized greeting
  • Quick links to primary workflows
  • Loading and empty states

6. AI symptom triage

Input

  • Typed symptom description
  • Regional-language selection
  • Browser speech recognition
  • Transcript review, edit and retry
  • Speech-confidence handling

Result

  • Urgency level
  • Recommended medical specialty
  • Plain-language explanation
  • Suggested next action
  • Safety guidance
  • Nearby clinic and appointment actions

Results are stored in triage history and can be reviewed or deleted. The AI is instructed not to diagnose or prescribe. Potential emergencies receive stronger escalation guidance.

AI triage workflow
Choose language→Type or speak symptoms→Review transcript→AI urgency analysis→Urgent?
Emergency guidance
Cautious guidance + specialist
Clinic search→Appointments→Saved history

Clinical wording: the summary contains patient-reported information, not a diagnosis. A certified clinician must verify it.

8. Medical Reports

  • Add, categorize, view and delete reports
  • Manual text entry, camera capture or image upload
  • OCR text extraction
  • AI plain-language simplification
  • Abnormal-value presentation and suggested specialist category
  • Language-aware explanation while preserving raw names, values, dates and units
  • Authenticated ownership protection

Report categories: blood test, urine test, X-ray, MRI, CT, ECG, ultrasound, prescription, discharge summary and other.

Report processing
Camera/file→Type and size validation→OCR→Patient review→AI simplification→Stored report

9. Prescriptions

  • Printed and handwritten prescription uploads
  • Camera/file input
  • Prescriber, date, medicine, strength, dosage, frequency, duration and instruction extraction
  • Confidence indicators and unclear-text warnings
  • Patient review/edit before saving
  • Prescription history with view, update and delete
  • Ownership protection
Unreadable text is marked “unclear” rather than invented. Extracted medicines do not automatically activate a medication schedule.
Prescription workflow
Upload image→OCR/handwriting extraction→Medicines + confidence→Patient confirms?→Save history

  • Browse and filter doctors by specialty and city

  • Update status or cancel
  • Google Maps clinic search

Appointment workflow
Specialist result or Appointments→Search specialty/city→Select doctor→Date/time + reason→Booked appointment

20. Current implementation boundaries

  • No hospital appointment-inventory integration
  • No payments or video consultations
  • No automated government-ID, police or medical-license verification
  • No clinic/hospital organization administration
  • No background push-notification service
  • Ambulance 108 integration remains halted
  • No comprehensive clinical audit-log system
  • Dependency-security remediation remains incomplete
Core implemented areas: patient and doctor portals, onboarding, multilingual navigation, accessibility aids, symptom triage, Pre-Consultation, OCR-assisted reports and prescriptions, health records, daily tracker, medications, appointments, child vaccination, Care Circle, caretaker workflows and emergency-access management.

Sushruth is a health-information and care-coordination assistant. AI-generated content must not be treated as diagnosis, medical advice or a substitute for evaluation by a qualified healthcare professional.
